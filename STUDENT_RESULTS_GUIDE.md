# 📊 Student Grades & Performance - How to View Your Results

## 🎯 Where to Find Your Exam Results

### From the Student Dashboard:

1. **Login** to your student account
2. Look for **"📊 My Exam Results"** in the navigation menu or sidebar
3. Click on it to view your grades and performance

---

## 📋 What You'll See

When you view your exam results, you'll see:

### For Each Exam:
- **Exam Code** - The unique code (e.g., MATH101-FIN)
- **Exam Title** - Name of the exam
- **Your Grade** - Letter grade (A+, A, B+, B, C+, C, D, F)
- **Marks Obtained** - Points you scored (e.g., 85/100)
- **Percentage** - Your score as a percentage (e.g., 85%)
- **Progress Bar** - Visual representation of your performance
- **Exam Date** - When the exam was held
- **Subject** - The subject area
- **Index Number** - Your student index/registration number

### Grade Breakdown:
- **A+** 🟢 (90-100%) - Outstanding performance
- **A** 🟢 (80-89%) - Excellent
- **B+** 🔵 (70-79%) - Very Good
- **B** 🔵 (60-69%) - Good
- **C+** 🟡 (50-59%) - Satisfactory
- **C** 🟡 (40-49%) - Acceptable
- **D** 🟠 (30-39%) - Poor
- **F** 🔴 (Below 30%) - Fail

## ❓ Why I Don't See My Results

If you don't see any results displayed, here are possible reasons:

### 1. **Results Haven't Been Uploaded Yet**
   - Ask your instructor to upload your exam results
   - Results must be uploaded by Admin/Instructor
   - Check with department for timeline

### 2. **Email Mismatch**
   - Your registered email doesn't match the email in results file
   - Contact admin to verify your email on file
   - Ensure email is spelled correctly

### 3. **Browser Cache Issue**
   - Clear your browser cache (Ctrl+Shift+Delete)
   - Close and reopen the application
   - Try a different browser

### 4. **Server Connection Issue**
   - Check if you're properly logged in
   - Try refreshing the page (F5)
   - Check your internet connection

### 5. **New Upload**
   - Results were just uploaded
   - Wait a few seconds and refresh (F5)
   - Some delay is normal

## 🔄 How Results Are Generated

### Process:
1. **Instructor uploads** exam results via Excel file
2. **System processes** the file and calculates grades
3. **Grades are assigned** automatically based on your marks
4. **Results appear** in your profile instantly
5. **You can view** your performance anytime

### Grading Formula:
- Percentage = (Marks Obtained / Total Marks) × 100
- Grade is assigned based on percentage ranges
