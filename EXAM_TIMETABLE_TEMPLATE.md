## Quick Fill Checklist

When adding a new exam, fill:

- ✅ **Title** - Full exam name (e.g., "Mathematics Final Examination")
- ✅ **Subject** - Subject name (e.g., "Mathematics")
- ✅ **Code** - Unique exam code (e.g., "MATH101-FIN")
- ✅ **Topic** - Topics covered (e.g., "Calculus, Algebra, Geometry")
- ✅ **Date** - Exam date (select from calendar)
- ✅ **Start Time** - Exam start time (e.g., "09:00")
- ✅ **End Time** - Exam end time (e.g., "11:00")
- ✅ **Duration** - Total duration in minutes (e.g., "120")
- ✅ **Total Marks** - Total marks (e.g., "100")
- ✅ **Year** - Academic year (2026, 2027, etc.)
- ✅ **Semester** - Semester (1, 2, or Special)
- ✅ **Status** - Exam status (Scheduled, Ongoing, Completed, Cancelled)
- 📝 **Description** - Optional details (exam format, special instructions)

## Notes

- Topic field is **optional** but recommended for student clarity
- Use commas to separate multiple topics
- Keep descriptions brief and clear
- Update exam status as it progresses (Scheduled → Ongoing → Completed)
